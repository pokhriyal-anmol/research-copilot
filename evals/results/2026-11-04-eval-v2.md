# Eval set v2: questions that name their paper

Written 2026-11-04.

## How it was made

`draft_questions.py` drafted 84 questions into `evals/questions-v2.csv`, one per paper, from the same deterministic passage sample as the first set. The prompt now requires the passage's own name for its method, system, model or dataset in the question; the first fix ("name the method") was read as "ask for the name" and was replaced after a 10-question trial. Gemini's free quota stopped drafting at 84 of a planned 120; `--continue` adds the rest later.

## How it was reviewed

At the owner's request the review was done by AI on their behalf, not by a person. The `reviewer` column says so on every row. A row is `reviewed=yes` only if all four of these agree:

- two reviewers, reading the question alone, say it names its paper (J and K agree on 79 of 84, Cohen's kappa 0.84);
- two reviewers, reading the question and full passage, say the passage answers it (L and M agree on 83 of 84).

Rows either pair split on are `no`, and so are two rows (36 and 59) that both answerability reviewers called only marginally answerable. Each reviewer gave a distinct, quoted reason per row, and each one's transcript shows it never opened another reviewer's file. Result: **56 of 84 reviewed `yes`**. Labels and reasons are in `2026-11-04-v2-identifiable-labels.json` and `2026-11-04-v2-answerable-labels.json`.

## Retrieval on it

The demo's retrieval, unchanged, scored by `python -m copilot.evaluate --questions <file>` on 2026-11-04. A hit is the question's own paper and page in the top 20.

| Question set | Questions | Recall@20, rerank | Margin | MRR, rerank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First set, all reviewed | 120 | 0.708 | ±0.081 | 0.528 |
| First set, the 44 that name their paper | 44 | 0.932 | ±0.074 | 0.694 |
| **Set v2, reviewed** | **56** | **0.893** | **±0.081** | **0.826** |

On questions that say what they are about, the right page is in the top 20 about 9 times in 10 on both sets, and set v2 puts it first much more often (MRR 0.826). The first set's 0.708 was mostly a measure of its questions. Answer-level numbers on set v2 (refusals, citation support) wait for the Gemini quota to reset.
